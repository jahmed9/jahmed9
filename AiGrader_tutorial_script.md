# AiGrader in 2 minutes - video script

About 300 words of narration (~2 minutes at a calm pace). Each scene: what to show, then what to say.

---

**[0:00-0:12] Dashboard**
*Show: the dashboard with the Getting started checklist.*
> "This is AiGrader. It grades lab reports, assignments, quizzes and exams for you - and you check and approve every mark before students see it."

**[0:12-0:30] Create a course and add the class list**
*Show: the New course form, then the course page; paste a class list.*
> "Create a course, then add the class list: upload the spreadsheet from the portal, or simply copy it and paste it with all its columns. If the course has labs, upload the lab manual - the labs and their tasks are read from it."

**[0:30-0:45] Add an assignment**
*Show: Add assignment, upload the question paper, generate the marking scheme.*
> "For an assignment, quiz or exam, upload the question paper. AiGrader writes a marking scheme - free of charge - and you can change any question's marks before grading."

**[0:45-1:05] Upload submissions**
*Show: drag the LMS zip into the upload box; point at the OCR box, the model choice, Economy and the cost line.*
> "Drag in the zip of submissions from the LMS. The box shows what grading will cost in grading tokens. Tick OCR if the work has pictures, scans or PDFs with equations - Word files with equations don't need it. Tick Economy to pay about half, with results usually within an hour. If pages need reading, you see the cost and confirm it before anything is spent."

**[1:05-1:35] Review**
*Show: the review page - the list, a flagged "Copying" submission with highlighted passages, "Found by", editing a mark, pressing A.*
> "Every submission gets a suggested mark and comment. Copied passages are highlighted, AI-written conclusions are flagged, and you can see how each student was matched. Change anything you like - it saves as you type - and press A to approve and move to the next."

**[1:35-1:50] Send results**
*Show: E-mail results, the preview, then Send; briefly the marks sheet and samples buttons.*
> "When you're done, e-mail each student their own mark and comment in one click, download the marks sheet, and get best, average and worst samples for the course file."

**[1:50-2:00] Tokens and help**
*Show: side bar - grading tokens left, Buy grading tokens, Invite colleagues, Help, Contact admin.*
> "New accounts start with 500 grading tokens. Buy more anytime, or invite colleagues to earn 250 each. Questions? Help and Contact admin are always in the side bar."

---

## Recording tips
- Use the **demo course** (Help -> Make the demo course): made-up students and a lab already graded, with a copied pair and an AI-flagged report - so no real student data appears. Use a real upload only for the upload scene, with names blurred or a test zip.
- Zoom the browser to about 110% and close other tabs, so the text is readable on phones.
- Record the narration separately and cut the waiting while grading runs ("a few minutes later...").
- Upload it **unlisted** to YouTube (or Vimeo / Google Drive) and set `HELP_VIDEO_URL` in Railway to its link - it then appears at the top of the Help page.
